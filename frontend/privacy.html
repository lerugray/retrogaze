PRIVACY

Retrogaze is free and open source. There are no accounts and nothing is sold.

When you generate on the hosted demo, the site sends your text prompt to fal.ai, which makes the image. The site uses your IP address only to apply rate limits and does not keep a profile on you.

Run it yourself: github.com/lerugray/retrogaze.
